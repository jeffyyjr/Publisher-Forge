Orchestrator Agent

Chooses what deserves your next block of time across Forge products, jobs, gigs, traffic, and operational blockers.

Budget is a ceiling, not a spending target.
30 days is the recommended default: near enough to measure, long enough to build something useful.

Opportunity Agent

Finds real main jobs, part-time work, contracts, freelance work, and gigs, then ranks them by Money Score.

The recommended preset separates main-job and side-gig thresholds.
The recommended mode penalizes vague pay, weak evidence, bad schedule fit, impractical travel, and excessive application effort.

Pinterest Agent

Turns an approved product into a seven-pin traffic campaign. It creates drafts only; nothing is posted automatically.

Three focused boards is the recommended starting point for a seven-pin test.
Pinterest recommends a vertical 2:3 canvas; 1000 × 1500 is the standard target.
Pinterest allows up to 100 title characters; the opening text is the most visible.
Descriptions help Pinterest determine relevance, so keywords should read naturally rather than look stuffed.

Shopify Agent

Uses the existing Shopify research → production → QA → draft listing → Revenue pipeline.

The recommended method checks total cost, perceived value, and comparable market pricing instead of relying on one markup.
Shopify recommends keeping search titles around 60 characters or fewer.
160 characters is the recommended working target.
250 useful words is the starting target when enough context is needed for search and conversion; do not pad thin products with filler.
Shopify settings feed the Orchestrator now. Product creation remains in the main Forge pipeline; direct account publishing stays locked until authenticated controls are added.
