## Verification gate for each approved clip

Before a clip can enter the buyer-facing package, record:

- file/path or durable approved-media reference;
- duration and capture date;
- exact feature(s) visibly demonstrated;
- whether the behavior is from the live product, a staging environment, or an edited mockup;
- any claim that must be qualified because a workflow is beta/adjacent rather than core;
- source/provenance of every included visual, music, voice, stock element, logo, and generated asset;
- transfer/license status for those elements;
- selected still timestamp(s) and a factual buyer-facing caption.

A clip fails the gate if the claimed behavior cannot be seen, if provenance is unknown, or if it implies revenue, customers, automation, marketplace publishing, or other capability beyond the evidence.

## Acquisition-video notes

Target a short buyer-oriented master rather than a hype reel. Recommended structure after verified footage exists:

- **0–10 sec:** what Publisher Forge is and who it is for;
- **10–35 sec:** live opportunity/research-to-project workflow;
- **35–60 sec:** production/QA/package workflow;
- **60–75 sec:** account/history or analytics evidence;
- **75–90 sec:** architecture/transferability and clear pre-revenue-beta disclosure.

Use on-screen captions that describe visible behavior. Avoid claims such as “fully autonomous publishing business,” “proven demand,” “profitable,” or “customers” unless later diligence evidence establishes them. Do not expose API keys, environment values, user records, email addresses, private analytics identifiers, or other sensitive data.
