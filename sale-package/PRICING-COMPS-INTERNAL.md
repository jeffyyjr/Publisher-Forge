# Publisher Forge — Comparable Market & Internal Pricing Notes

Internal seller material — not buyer-facing.

Research date: 2026-09-22

## Important valuation boundary

Publisher Forge is currently a live **pre-revenue beta** with limited external traction. Revenue/profit multiples from established SaaS businesses are therefore context, not a valid direct valuation formula for Publisher Forge. Public marketplace prices below are asking prices unless explicitly identified otherwise; an asking price is not evidence of a completed transaction at that price.

## Current market evidence

### Marketplace / market-level evidence

- **Acquire.com, Jan. 2026 acquisition-multiples report:** Acquire reports that confirmed SaaS sales in both 2024 and 2025 had a median **3.9x profit** multiple and explicitly notes that asking prices are often higher than final outcomes. Source: https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-jan-2026/
- **Acquire.com, 2026 State of the Deal:** the marketplace describes current SaaS ranges around **3–5x profit or 1–3x revenue**, dependent on profitability, growth, churn, scale and risk. These metrics primarily describe operating businesses, not pre-revenue software assets. Source: https://blog.acquire.com/business-acquisitions-2026-webinar/
- **Acquire asking-price guidance:** pricing should be justified by verifiable facts; useful code/IP can support value, while younger/unscaled businesses generally warrant lower multiples. Source: https://help.acquire.com/how-to-set-your-asking-price
- An older Acquire guide specifically advised pre-revenue or sub-$5,000 TTM-revenue startups to price **below $25,000** to attract buyers. It is dated evidence rather than a 2026 transaction comp, but it remains useful as a sanity check for a pre-revenue asset. Source: https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-feb-2023/

## How Publisher Forge compares

### Value-positive attributes

- Working private codebase and live Render deployment rather than a concept/mockup.
- Meaningful publishing workflow: generation, QA, packaging/export, account/quota infrastructure and supporting operational paths.
- Transfer documentation and sale-specific diligence package are being prepared before listing.
- Persistent storage and deployment architecture are documented.
- Product can be acquired as code/IP and extended without acquiring a large organization or payroll.

### Value-negative / risk attributes

- Pre-revenue; no defensible ARR, MRR or profit multiple exists.
- Limited beta traffic and no established paid-customer cohort or retention history.
- Billing is not a completed production capability.
- Some adjacent workflows are beta/experimental rather than equally mature core product paths.
- Final treatment of production user data, brand/social assets and demo-media licenses remains a diligence decision.
- A buyer still assumes product-market-fit, distribution and monetization risk.

## Internal pricing position

Pricing should be framed as an **asset/IP acquisition**, not as a revenue-multiple SaaS acquisition.

### Recommended initial asking range: **$15,000–$22,500**

A practical initial list price is **$19,500**.

Rationale:

1. It stays below the historical Acquire $25k sanity threshold for pre-revenue/sub-$5k-TTM startups rather than pretending Publisher Forge deserves revenue-producing SaaS multiples.
2. It sits above the current $9k SvitAI asking-price observation because Publisher Forge has a live deployment, a broader publishing workflow, transfer documentation and an increasingly organized acquisition package; this is a seller judgment, not proof of market value.
3. It remains dramatically below current $60k–$130k+ AI SaaS asking prices where sellers show actual ARR/customers.
4. $19.5k leaves room for normal negotiation while remaining within a plausible individual-builder/micro-acquirer budget.

### Private negotiation floor: **$10,000 cash-equivalent**

Do not publish this floor. Below $10k, default to declining unless deal structure creates concrete additional value (for example, unusually fast/clean closing with buyer assuming migration work and no seller financing). Do not count vague earn-outs, speculative future royalties, or nonbinding promises at face value when comparing against the floor.

### Preferred negotiation bands

- **$18k–$22.5k:** strong result for the current evidence level; prioritize clean terms and short transition.
- **$15k–$18k:** reasonable clearing zone if buyer diligence is straightforward.
- **$10k–$15k:** only accept after weighing speed/certainty, included assets, support burden and payment structure.
- **Below $10k:** below internal floor absent exceptional, quantifiable consideration.

## What could justify repricing upward before sale

Revisit the range rather than automatically raising it if Publisher Forge establishes any of the following with evidence:

- paying customers and recurring revenue;
- repeat `quota_consumed` usage from independent users;
- credible activation/retention cohorts;
- an attributable acquisition channel producing signups and product use;
- completed billing with evidence of successful transactions;
- transferable domain/brand/social assets with demonstrated audience value.

Conversely, unresolved security issues, licensing uncertainty, data-transfer complications, broken production workflows or substantial buyer-required rebuild work should push pricing downward.

## Seller positioning

Lead with **“working AI publishing software asset with live deployment and documented handoff”**, not “profitable SaaS,” “proven business,” or unsupported claims about autonomous KDP publishing. The sale thesis is that a buyer gets working code, workflow/IP, deployment know-how and a head start on commercialization—not an established revenue stream.
