# Publisher Forge — Selling Channels & Launch Order

Updated: 2026-09-27

Internal seller-use document.

## Recommended launch order

### 1. Acquire.com — primary marketplace

Publisher Forge is a plausible fit for Acquire's current pre-revenue SaaS/AI exception because it is:

- a live functional SaaS/AI product;
- publicly deployed;
- able to support signup/login;
- positioned around a defined publishing/digital-product workflow;
- intended to be transferable;
- priced at **$19,500**, below Acquire's commonly stated $25,000 pre-revenue threshold.

Acquire still decides acceptance case by case. Its current guidance says pre-revenue products need to be functional, transferable, clearly positioned and realistically priced, and it specifically notes that a product walkthrough/demo improves confidence and approval likelihood.

Sources:
- https://help.acquire.com/what-pre-revenue-businesses-are-allowed
- https://help.acquire.com/seller-faqs-1
- https://help.acquire.com/how-to-list-my-startup

**Action when video is ready:** submit Publisher Forge to Acquire first using `FINAL-SALE-PACKAGE.md`, attach the verified walkthrough, then populate the private data room from `BUYER-DATA-ROOM-INDEX.md`.

### 2. Direct founder / operator outreach — run in parallel

Use `OUTREACH-PACK.md` for targeted outreach to:

- AI-SaaS founders;
- creator/publishing software companies;
- KDP/digital-product operators;
- agencies;
- micro-acquirers;
- technical founders who buy code/IP before monetization.

Direct outreach is important because Publisher Forge is being sold primarily as a working software asset, not on an MRR multiple.

### 3. Flippa — secondary marketplace

Flippa actively supports SaaS acquisitions and emphasizes clear documentation, transferability, due diligence and realistic valuation. Its public 2026 material is weighted toward revenue-producing SaaS, so treat Flippa as a secondary channel rather than assuming the same pre-revenue fit as Acquire.

Sources:
- https://flippa.com/blog/how-to-sell-a-saas-company/
- https://flippa.com/blog/what-documents-do-i-need-to-sell-a-business-full-list/

**Action:** test listing eligibility after the Acquire submission is prepared. Use the same truthful pre-revenue positioning and do not add unsupported revenue/user claims simply to fit marketplace expectations.

### 4. Microns — do not use while pre-revenue

Microns' current seller page says it does **not** accept pre-revenue projects and requires traction/revenue. Publisher Forge should not be submitted there in its current stage.

Source:
- https://www.microns.io/sell-your-startup

Reconsider only if Publisher Forge gains verified paying customers/revenue before the sale closes.

## Go-live sequence

1. Finish and verify the buyer demo.
2. Insert demo/still references in the sale package.
3. Submit Acquire listing.
4. Begin direct outreach the same day.
5. Test Flippa eligibility/listing.
6. Track all inquiries and offers.
7. Share private diligence only with qualified buyers.
8. Compare offers by cash-equivalent value and support burden, not headline number.
9. Close with an explicit asset schedule and buyer-controlled credentials/deployment.

## Do not do

- Do not submit to Microns while pre-revenue.
- Do not call beta traffic “customers” or “demand.”
- Do not invent MRR or profit.
- Do not publish the private negotiation floor.
- Do not send source code or production-user data in first-contact outreach.
- Do not include personal credentials in any data room.
