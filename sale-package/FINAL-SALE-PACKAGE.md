### What Publisher Forge does

Publisher Forge turns a product idea into a structured workflow:

1. Research live public opportunity signals.
2. Evaluate and rank the opportunity.
3. Generate a product brief.
4. Produce original content and marketplace metadata.
5. Run Quality Control.
6. Build and validate customer-facing files with Release QA.
7. Package publishing-oriented deliverables.
8. Measure launch and growth signals for iteration.
