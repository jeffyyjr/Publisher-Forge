# Publisher Forge — Buyer Outreach Pack

Updated: 2026-09-27

Internal seller-use document. Do not include the private negotiation floor in buyer-facing outreach.

## Positioning

Primary position:

**Working AI publishing software asset with a live deployment and documented handoff.**

Do not lead with “business,” “MRR,” “customers,” or “passive income.” Publisher Forge is pre-revenue and should be sold on build-time compression, code/IP, product depth and acquisition optionality.

## Target buyer profiles

Prioritize people or companies that can monetize a working codebase faster than the current founder:

1. AI-SaaS founders with an existing audience or distribution channel.
2. Creator-tool / publishing-tool operators.
3. KDP, Etsy or digital-product operators who want proprietary software.
4. Agencies that could use the workflow internally and sell services around it.
5. Micro-acquirers comfortable with pre-revenue software assets.
6. Technical founders looking for a substantial head start rather than a blank MVP.

Avoid spending much time on buyers who require proven MRR, large customer cohorts or seller financing as a prerequisite.

## Serious-buyer response

Absolutely. I’ll keep the process straightforward.

Start with the live product and buyer memo. If the product scope matches what you are looking for, the next diligence layer covers the architecture, deployment, tests/security evidence, known limitations, data-transfer boundaries and asset checklist.

The current asking price is **$19,500**. Existing production user data is not assumed to transfer, and personal credentials/API keys are excluded. The intended handoff is a clean buyer-controlled deployment with fresh credentials unless the purchase agreement says otherwise.

## “Why $19,500 if there is no revenue?” response

The price is not based on an MRR multiple. It is an asset/IP price for a live deployed codebase and the build time it compresses.

A buyer is getting the research engine, publishing workflow, account layer, QA/release logic, packaging/export system, analytics instrumentation, security/test automation, deployment documentation and several expansion modules already implemented.

The buyer still takes monetization and distribution risk, which is why the asset is priced well below revenue-producing SaaS listings.

## “What is your lowest price?” response

I’m open to reasonable offers based on the structure and speed of the deal, but I’m not negotiating against myself before a buyer has reviewed the asset and diligence package. Send me the terms you have in mind after you’ve looked at the product.

## “Can you prove revenue?” response

No established revenue is being represented. Publisher Forge is being sold as a pre-revenue software asset. The diligence package separates verified product/deployment evidence from limited beta traffic so a buyer can value the code and product foundation without relying on unsupported commercial claims.

## “Is it fully autonomous?” response

No blanket fully-autonomous claim is being made. The strongest core paths are research, production, QA, release validation, packaging, accounts and measurement. Some marketplace/social actions remain guided, approval-based or beta workflows, and the sale materials identify those boundaries.

## “Does it include your API keys/accounts?” response

No personal credentials transfer. The buyer should deploy with buyer-controlled OpenAI, GitHub, hosting and optional provider credentials. The handoff documentation is designed around fresh credentials and a clean cutover.

## Qualification questions for interested buyers

Use these after interest is established:

- Do you acquire pre-revenue software assets, or only businesses with MRR?
- Are you primarily interested in the publishing workflow, the broader agent platform, or both?
- Would you want a clean buyer-controlled redeployment, or do you need the current hosted service transferred?
- What diligence do you require before making an offer?
- What closing timeline and payment structure do you normally use?

Do not reveal the private negotiation floor during qualification.
