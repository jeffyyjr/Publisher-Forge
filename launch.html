FAQ

Before you try it.

Is this an automatic Amazon publishing bot?

No. Forge automates research, production, QA, packaging, and guided handoff where it can do so reliably. Marketplace publishing still keeps human approval in the loop.

Does Forge guarantee a product will sell?

No. Trend evidence and scoring are decision support, not verified sales or a revenue guarantee. The Revenue Agent exists specifically to measure what actually happened.

Does it only work for books?

Publishing is the most mature path today. Shopify, Pinterest, opportunity search, viral remix, security, and orchestration are active beta layers in the same project.

How much does the beta cost?

The current goal is to collect real usage and feedback before charging. Pricing and paid plans are not live yet.
