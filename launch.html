FAQ

Before you try it.

Do I have to create an account first?

No. One live Trend Radar scan is available before signup. An account is offered afterward so you can save the research, run more scans, and continue into the rest of Forge.

Does Forge guarantee a product will sell?

No. Trend evidence and scoring are decision support, not verified sales or a revenue guarantee. The point is to make a better-informed test before spending meaningful time or money.

What happens after I find an idea?

If you want to continue, Publisher Forge can move an opportunity through analysis, production, release QA, marketplace packaging, and measured-results tracking.

How much does the beta cost?

The current beta is free with daily usage allowances so Forge can collect real usage and feedback without exposing unlimited AI and video costs. Pricing and paid plans are not live yet.
